Privacy Policy — SolarVolt Pro

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SolarVolt Pro, developed by SILKYLINEAI, respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No account is required and no personal data is collected by the developer.
  • All calculations (solar production, DC and AC load, battery backup) are done on your device. Your inputs stay on your device.
  • Location is used only on your device to fetch local weather and temperature for efficiency estimates. Your location is never sent to the developer.
  • The app does not show third-party ads and does not use analytics or tracking.
  • The app may request internet access only to fetch weather data.
